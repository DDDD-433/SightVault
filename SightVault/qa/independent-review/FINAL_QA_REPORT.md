Codex independently reproduced Opus's 12 failing cases, reviewed Sonnet's cases, implemented fixes, and removed strict-xfail markers only for resolved behavior. **Integrated regression result: 123 passed, 1 expected failure.** Evidence: `tests-final.log`. The expected failure is S-6: saved copies from older edited versions are retained on disk. Automatic garbage collection was not added because those copies may be useful for recovery; this remains a low-severity storage-growth limitation. No claim of a completely defect-free product is made.
